# 🎨 Animation Guide - How to Experience All Effects

## 🖱️ Mouse Interactions

### 1. **3D Tilt Cards**
- **Where**: Certification cards, skill categories, impact cards
- **How**: Move your mouse over any card
- **Effect**: Card tilts in 3D following your cursor position
- **Tip**: Move slowly to see the smooth perspective change

### 2. **Magnetic Buttons**
- **Where**: "View My Work" and "Download Resume" buttons, contact buttons
- **How**: Hover near buttons
- **Effect**: Buttons slightly move toward your cursor
- **Tip**: Circle around the button to see the magnetic pull

### 3. **Tech Badge Animations**
- **Where**: Technology tags under experience items
- **How**: Hover over any tech badge
- **Effect**: Badge scales up, rotates, and changes color with ripple
- **Tip**: Hover quickly over multiple badges for a wave effect

### 4. **Navigation Underline**
- **Where**: Top navigation menu
- **How**: Hover over menu items
- **Effect**: Smooth underline grows from center
- **Tip**: The active section is automatically highlighted

### 5. **Cursor Trail**
- **Where**: Entire page
- **How**: Move your mouse anywhere
- **Effect**: Gradient particles follow your cursor
- **Tip**: Move in circles or figure-8 patterns for cool trails

## 📜 Scroll Interactions

## 🏆 Pro Tips for Best Experience

1. **Use Chrome or Edge**: Best animation performance
2. **Full Screen**: Press F11 for immersive experience
3. **Smooth Scrolling**: Use mouse wheel, not scrollbar
4. **Take Your Time**: Scroll slowly to see all effects
5. **Hover Everything**: Every element has some interaction
6. **Mobile**: Touch and scroll work too, but mouse is best
7. **Refresh**: Some animations only play on load
8. **Wait**: Some effects are time-based (5-second intervals)

## 🎨 Animation Performance

- **60 FPS**: All animations run at 60 frames per second
- **GPU Accelerated**: Using CSS transforms for smooth performance
- **Optimized**: Intersection Observer for efficient scroll detection
- **Responsive**: All animations work on mobile (touch-adapted)

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

**Animations not smooth?**
- Close other browser tabs
- Update your browser
- Check GPU acceleration is enabled

**Some effects not working?**
- Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
- Clear browser cache
- Try different browser

**Want to disable animations?**
- Add `prefers-reduced-motion` in browser settings
- Or comment out script.js
